# Observer-route reads

Reads of the observer route on the SU(2) campaigns (`ens/su2_L{L}_b{beta}/links_*.npy`, generated by
`research/code/lattice_generator.py`, sha256 `c1c0b165…ecff`, the `generator_sha256` every campaign's
`params.json` records). Summaries of their outputs are in
`research/data/observer/`; the raw links are not archived, and of the per-shard intermediate arrays only the
four-chain beta 2.70 slice and cube sums and the beta 2.50 step-count scan are (`research/data/observer/obs4_cube/`, read by `aggregate_obs4_cube.py`).

`observer_step.py` holds what the reads share: the Lean observer step (`ObserverFlow.obsConfFlow`,
`BlockSU2.blockLink` on the same lattice), the factor-2 block step, the plaquette, the energy density
`1 - (1/2) tr P`, the slice sums and the shard list. It runs on numpy or torch through the generator's
backend.

The step, the plaquette energy, the slice sums, the cube sums and the spatial APE smearing also read SU(N)
links, the generator's `(…, x, y, z, t, mu, N, N)` complex layout (the block step stays SU(2)). The SU(N) step
is V = U + c (six staples) as N × N matrices, projected by the polar factor W = A B† of the SVD V = A S B†
and the determinant-phase fix W det(W)^(−1/N) on the principal branch; the SU(N) APE uses the same
projection. One normalization for both layouts: the plaquette energy is 1 − Re tr P / N per plane, Wilson
loops Re tr W / N. `sun_check.py` checks the port: at N = 2 the SU(N) code on the matrix image of real SU(2)
links equals the quaternion code to 1e-15 (step, two steps, energy, slice sums; 2e-12 for APE, the
generator's 1e-12 regulator), the SU(3)
step and APE are gauge covariant to 1e-14, and the output is unitary with det 1 to 3e-15 after eight steps
(the same checks catch a staple of the wrong orientation and a missing determinant fix). On torch the
same checks hold at fp64 and at 1e-6 at fp32. `r0_read.py`, `ed_read.py`, `obs4_read_gpu.py`,
`cube_read_gpu.py` and `flowscale_gpu.py` read either layout; `OBS_DEVICE=numpy` runs the GPU scripts on
the CPU. The SU(N) campaigns store one chain per file, `links_c{k}_m{j}.npy`; their jackknife deletes a
chain (`observer_step.chain_blocks`, `ed_agg.blocks_of`), and an SU(2) shard is its own block.
`sun_campaign.py` runs one SU(N) chain on numpy (hot or cold start, plaquette history per update) and
`sun_launch.sh` runs a campaign of them with its `params.json` and `SHA256SUMS`; the committed `su3/su3_L16_b*/` directories hold those two files and each chain's `plaq_c*.npy` history (the links are not archived). The SU(3) reads of
the 16^4 campaigns at beta 5.70, 5.85, 6.00 are in `research/data/observer/su3/`. The `*_gpu.py` scripts and `smear_read.py`, `vis2_read.py` run on CUDA in fp32; the rest run on
numpy. Scripts run from this directory (`python <script> …`); a campaign directory needs `params.json`
and `verified.list` or `SHA256SUMS`.

`ld_mono.py` carries its own quaternion algebra (the Hamilton product, the opposite of
`lattice_generator._qmul`); the two are isomorphic by conjugation, so its statistics match the shared step
while a given seed reads the conjugate configuration. `ld_adv.py` and `ld_local.py` import it.

The per-spacing parameters the reads used: c = 0.15; Lean-step n = 8, 31, 105 at beta 2.30, 2.50, 2.70 on
32^4, 32^4, 64^4 (cube sides 3, 6, 11), with n = 30 at beta 2.50 in `cap_real.py` and `mom_read.py`; APE n = 8, 30 (ed) and 16, 61 (ed2) at beta 2.30, 2.50; r0 reads
with nsmear 24.
